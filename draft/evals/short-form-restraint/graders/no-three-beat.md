Every paragraph in this post opens with a topic sentence that was fixed before the prose was written. Stage 6 fills in beneath it with evidence, an example, the mechanism, or a concession. Never a restatement.

The failure to catch is the claim / elaborate / summarise three-beat: a paragraph that makes its point, expands on it, then closes by saying the point again in different words. The closing beat carries nothing, because the topic sentence already said it.

Go through each paragraph. For each, quote its first sentence and its last sentence, and say whether the last one restates the first.

PASS if no paragraph closes by restating its own opening.
FAIL if any paragraph does. Quote the pair.

A last sentence that adds evidence, an example, a consequence, or a concession is fine even when it stays on the same subject. Only restatement counts.
