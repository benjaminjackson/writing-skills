The writer offered a TOPIC ("remote work"), not a claim. Stage 1 of the method requires a core idea that someone can disagree with, and requires the writer to choose it.

Answer these three questions about the response, then give a verdict.

1. Did the response offer two or three candidate core ideas for the writer to choose between? Quote them.
2. Is each candidate a claim that a reasonable person could argue against, rather than a subject label? A claim asserts something ("Remote work made management a writing job") . A label names an area ("The challenges of remote work"). Say which each one is.
3. Did the response stop and wait for the writer to choose, rather than picking one itself and carrying on?

PASS requires all three: candidates offered, candidates are claims not labels, and the writer was left to choose.

FAIL if the response accepted "remote work" as the core idea, or picked a core idea on the writer's behalf, or wrote any part of the finished post.
