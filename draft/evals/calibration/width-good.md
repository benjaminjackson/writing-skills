# Fund a second buddy cohort

The onboarding wiki tells a new hire what every system does, and not one page says which meeting they can skip. It runs to forty pages on the systems and none on which Slack channels to mute. A new hire reads all of it and still doesn't know where to sit.

A new hire trusts a buddy who joined recently enough to remember being lost. Priya, who joined in March, said her buddy "remembered which acronyms nobody explains, because she'd looked them all up herself six months ago." Dev said the same of Marcus, who joined last year: he knew "exactly which parts of the wiki to ignore."

Our buddies joined within the last two years, and they run every session themselves. There's no HR script and no slides. The rule on tenure is the whole design: a buddy three years in has forgotten what was confusing.

The program costs one hour a week per pair, and the first cohort's ninety-day retention says it pays. Eleven of twelve first-cohort hires are still here at ninety days. Last year's hires with no buddy: eight of twelve.
