The merge is not what broke on-call, because one rotation only fails when the person holding the pager cannot act on what it says. A rotation is a schedule. It does not confer knowledge.

A platform engineer cannot action a payments page, so every payments alert turns into an escalation to the one person who knows those services. The runbooks are platform only; payments has a wiki page from 2023 with half its links dead.

That leaves one engineer permanently on call, leave included, which is the exact failure a rotation exists to prevent. Dana took three weeks of leave last quarter and was called on two of them. As she put it in the retro: "I don't mind being the expert, I mind being the only one."

Splitting the rotations back would hide that problem, and writing the payments runbooks would end it.
