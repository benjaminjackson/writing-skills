# What the list actually holds

## The list is shorter than it looks

Your list is shorter than it looks, and the number that survives the cut is the number that decides the next eight weeks. You've got 15 organizations on it and 4 have closed, so 11 are live. That's the real size of the next eight weeks.

## Most of it is already reachable

Most of what survives is already reachable through somebody you know, and a small handful of your contacts covers all of it. 8 of the 11 live ones have a named contact one hop away, and just 5 people account for all 8. That's a better ratio than almost anyone gets.

## The rest are a visibility problem

The 3 live ones with nobody one hop away are a visibility problem rather than an introduction problem, and they need slower work. There's no one to ask, so the fix is being seen instead of being forwarded.

## So the plan sizes itself

So the plan sizes itself: one introduction request a week against the 8 reachable ones, and a longer game on the other 3.
