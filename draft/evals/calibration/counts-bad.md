# What the list actually holds

## The list is shorter than it looks

Your list is shorter than it looks, and the number that survives the cut is the number that decides the next eight weeks. You've got fourteen organizations on it, and two of them ran a hiring cycle for your role and closed it in the last five months. That leaves twelve you can actually work.

## Most of it is already reachable

Most of what survives is already reachable through somebody you know, and a small handful of your contacts covers all of it. Ten of the twelve have someone in your network one hop away, which is a better ratio than almost anyone gets.

## The rest are a visibility problem

The four with nobody one hop away are a visibility problem rather than an introduction problem, and they need slower work. There's no one to ask, so the fix is being seen instead of being forwarded.

## So the plan sizes itself

So the plan sizes itself: one introduction request a week against the ten reachable ones, and a longer game on the other four.
