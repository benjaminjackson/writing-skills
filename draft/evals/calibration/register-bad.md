# Search Plan

Three of your four conversations in six weeks came from people you already knew. That's the shape of the next eight weeks, not an accident of this one. Six weeks is a small window and three out of four is a small sample, but it matches every search I've run at this level.

The purpose of a professional network is to create durable channels of mutual value over an extended period. It is important to understand that such relationships are established through consistent engagement rather than through transactional outreach at the point of need. Organizations increasingly recognize that referred candidates demonstrate higher retention and stronger performance outcomes.

It is therefore essential to approach each introduction with clarity regarding the value proposition being offered. A well-constructed request enables the intermediary to advocate effectively on the candidate's behalf, and reduces the burden placed upon them.

The practical version is short. Write the introduction email yourself, in the third person, so whoever forwards it can paste it and add one line. Two paragraphs, no attachments.

Week ten will go quiet whatever you do. That's arithmetic, not a signal, and the only wrong response is to start applying cold to make the quiet stop.
