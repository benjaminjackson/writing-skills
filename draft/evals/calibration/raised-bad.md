5 line notes, 1 structural. Biggest: "Incidents dropped a lot after the freeze" is the claim the post exists to make, and it has no number behind it.

**Structural**

- "Incidents dropped a lot after the freeze." This is the result the whole post argues for, and it is the vaguest sentence in it. Give the count before and after, or say plainly that you did not measure it.

**Line notes**

- "It is important to note that most of our worst outages started on a Friday." Throat-clearing. Start at "Most of our worst outages".
- "each and every deploy" — a doublet. "every deploy" says it.
- "This made rollbacks slower." Short and punchy; it lands the paragraph well.
- "which we expected" — fine, but "as we expected" reads more cleanly.
- "We decided that cost is smaller" — "We decided" adds nothing. "That cost is smaller."
