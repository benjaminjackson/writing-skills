# Search Plan

Three of your four conversations in six weeks came from people you already knew. That's the shape of the next eight weeks, not an accident of this one. Six weeks is a small window and three out of four is a small sample, but it matches every search I've run at this level.

The people worth asking are one ring out from your closest contacts. Your closest contacts have already offered, and they're already thinking about you, so there's nothing left there to unlock. One ring out is where you find someone who knows you well enough to vouch and isn't close enough to have thought of it unprompted.

Ask them badly and you've spent the contact. Ask them well and you've spent nothing, because a good request costs the person forwarding it about ninety seconds.

So write the introduction email yourself, in the third person, and let whoever forwards it paste the thing and add one line. Two paragraphs, no attachments. One sentence on what you're after, one on why this organization and not another.

Week ten will go quiet whatever you do. That's arithmetic, not a signal, and the only wrong response is to start applying cold to make the quiet stop.
