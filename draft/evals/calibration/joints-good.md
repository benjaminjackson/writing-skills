# Search Plan

Three of your four conversations came from people you already knew. The recruiter brought one, your own network brought three, and that ratio has held every week since we started. The one the recruiter brought is also the only one that has gone nowhere.

The recruiter process is the one to stop paying for. You met a screener, then a second screener, and the role description moved twice between the two calls. Nobody in that chain can tell you what the job actually is, which is a problem you can't fix from outside it. The list you can fix is the other one.

Twelve of your fourteen organizations are still live. Two have run a hiring cycle for your role and closed it in the last five months, so they come off. Ten of the remaining twelve have someone in your network one hop away, and that one hop is worth more than anything else on this page.

A warm introduction beats a strong application because it changes who reads your name first. An application starts with a screener whose job is to cut a pile on titles and years. Very little of your last eleven years survives that cut.
