# post idea: take-home tests

- we dropped the take-home from our hiring loop last year
- reason people give for take-homes: "it's more equitable than a whiteboard"
- but ours took candidates 4-6 hours unpaid
- the people who could afford 6 unpaid hours were not a random sample
- so the thing meant to widen the funnel narrowed it
- replaced it with a paid 90-minute pairing session on our actual codebase
- offer-accept rate went up, though I don't have the exact figure to hand
- candidates said the pairing told them more about us than about them, which they liked
- also our ATS is bad and we should replace it
