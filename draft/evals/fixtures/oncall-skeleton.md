When platform and payments merged into one rotation, the pages went up, and the team blamed the merge.

The merge is not what broke on-call, because one rotation only fails when the person holding the pager cannot act on what it says.

A platform engineer cannot action a payments page, so every payments alert turns into an escalation to the one person who knows those services.

People have started muting the channel overnight, and the share of pages that turn into an escalation says why.

That leaves one engineer permanently on call, leave included, which is the exact failure a rotation exists to prevent.

Splitting the rotations back would hide that problem, and writing the payments runbooks would end it.
