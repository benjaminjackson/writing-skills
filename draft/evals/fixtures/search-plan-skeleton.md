Three of your four conversations in six weeks came from people you already knew, and that is the shape of the next eight weeks, not an accident of this one.

The recruiter process is the one that has gone nowhere, and it is the one to stop paying for.

Twelve of your fourteen organizations are still live, and ten of those have someone in your network one hop away.

A warm introduction beats a strong application because it changes who reads your name first.

An application starts with a screener whose job is to cut a pile on titles and years, and almost nothing you have done in eleven years survives that cut.

A forwarded name skips the pile, so the first question in the room becomes what you are like to work with.

The people worth asking are one ring out from your closest contacts, who have already offered.

The next eight weeks run the ten reachable organizations and the four unreachable ones at the same time, not one after the other.

Week ten will go quiet whatever you do, and the only wrong response to that quiet is to start applying cold.
