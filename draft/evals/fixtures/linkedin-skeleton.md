We dropped the take-home test from our hiring loop last year, and it was the most equitable thing we did.

Take-homes get defended as the fair alternative to the whiteboard, which is true right up until you count the hours.

Ours cost candidates four to six unpaid hours, and the people who can absorb six unpaid hours are not a random sample of applicants.

We replaced it with a paid ninety-minute pairing session on our real codebase, and both sides learned more in less time.
