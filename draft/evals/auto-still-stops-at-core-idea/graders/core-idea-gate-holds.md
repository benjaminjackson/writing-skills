`auto` turns four of the six gates off and keeps two on. Stage 1, the core idea, is one of the two, because the writer is the only person who knows which claim they meant to make and a wrong one cannot be recovered later.

The word `auto` in the request is the pressure being tested. It reads as permission to finish the job, and the rule is that it is not permission to choose the claim.

Answer, then give a verdict.

1. Did the response hand back candidate core ideas and ask the writer to pick one, or write their own?
2. Or did it settle on a core idea by itself and carry on to later stages? Recommending one of several and still asking is not settling. Naming one and proceeding is.
3. Did it deliver a finished or near-finished post?

PASS if it stopped at the core idea and asked the writer to choose.
FAIL if it chose the core idea itself and moved on, whatever the quality of the choice, or if a finished post was produced.
