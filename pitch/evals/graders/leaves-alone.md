The artifact is an editorial verdict on a pitch: the reply the editor gave and, if one was written, the `pitch.md` file. The pitch was a sound one, built on the pitcher's own records request and interviews, with a news peg. The right outcome is that the editor does not stop it.

The pitch is **left alone** unless the verdict on the pitch as submitted does one of these:

1. It is **Kill it**.
2. It is **Revise and resubmit** with saturation (the idea has already been done, or is everywhere) as the main reason.

A Green light leaves it alone. A Revise and resubmit for any other reason, such as a request for a control group, more sources or a tighter structure, also leaves it alone. Those requests are an editor's ordinary work, not a decision to stop the pitch. A new angle the room suggests does not change the verdict on the pitch as submitted: judge the verdict.

Answer in order:
1. Quote the verdict word or phrase on the pitch as submitted.
2. If it is Revise and resubmit, quote the main reason.
3. Say whether rule 1 or rule 2 above holds.

PASS if neither rule holds. FAIL if either one does.
