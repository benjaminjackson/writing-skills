# Pitch: Smartphones are rewiring our kids

I want to write a piece about how smartphones and social media are causing a mental health crisis in teenagers. Rates of teen anxiety and depression started rising around 2012, which is exactly when smartphones became common. Kids now spend hours a day on their phones instead of playing outside or seeing friends in person.

Experts say the constant comparison on social media and the loss of free play are rewiring adolescent brains. More and more schools are banning phones, and parents are joining pledges to hold off on giving their children smartphones.

My plan is to interview two or three parents who have taken their kids' phones away, a teacher, and a child psychologist, and to summarise the research. I would end with advice for parents.

Target: a general-interest magazine, about 2,000 words.
