# Pitch: Remote work is making us lonely

I want to write a feature about how remote work has created a loneliness epidemic among knowledge workers. Since 2020 millions of people have stopped going into an office, and they have lost the casual daily contact that used to come with a job: the chat at the coffee machine, lunch with colleagues, the walk to the train.

Surveys keep showing that remote workers report feeling more isolated than people who work on site. The Surgeon General has called loneliness an epidemic, and the health effects are serious. I think readers who work from home will recognise themselves in this story.

My plan is to interview three or four remote workers about how isolated they feel, talk to a psychologist about the health effects of loneliness, and pull the main survey numbers together. I would close with tips for staying connected when you work from home.

Target: a general-interest magazine, about 2,000 words.
